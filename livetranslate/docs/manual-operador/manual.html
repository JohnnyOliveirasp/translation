How to run the live translation during the service — step by step, with pictures of every screen and every button you will press.

Redeem Community Church

What this system does While the pastor preaches, people in the congregation hear the sermon live in Spanish or Portuguese on their own phones, through their earbuds. Your job is simple: start it before the service, glance at it now and then, and end it after the service. The system does the rest — it even mutes itself automatically during worship music.

4If something looks wrong

Almost everything fixes itself. Here is the short list, in order of how likely it is:

What you see / hearWhat to do
Someone says: “I see the captions but the sound stopped” on their phone Their phone paused the audio (common on iPhones). Tell them to tap the big “TAP TO RESUME SOUND” button shown on their screen — or leave and re-pick their language. Nothing is wrong on your side.
Preaching has started but the card is still amber “Muted — worship” for more than ~15 seconds Click Always translating. When the next worship set starts, click Automatic again.
Pastor is chatting near a live mic before the service and you don’t want that translated Click Always muted. Click Automatic when the service starts.
A red error message appears, or the level meter stays at zero while the pastor speaks Refresh the page (F5), check the microphone dropdown, and click Go live again. Listeners just re-pick their language — takes them 5 seconds.
You closed the tab by accident Open livetranslate.church/broadcast/redeem-community-church again and click Go live. Same as above.
🚫 Please don’t • Don’t close the laptop lid or let it sleep while ON AIR.
• Don’t click End broadcast in the middle of the service.
• Don’t change anything under Detector tuning — those sliders are pre-calibrated.

Quick reference — the whole Sunday in 6 lines

Computer on power · sound-board USB cable connected · open Chrome
livetranslate.church/login → email + password → Log in
Open broadcast → Microphone = sound board (USB) → Go live → Allow mic
During service: green = translating, amber during worship = normal. Leave it alone.
Stuck on “Muted — worship” during preaching? → Always translating, then back to Automatic
After the service: End broadcast. Done — the sermon PDFs are created automatically.
Need help during the service? Text or call Johnny — and don’t worry: the worst case is refreshing the page and clicking Go live again. You cannot break anything.
