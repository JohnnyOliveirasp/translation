Live Translation for Our Services

While you preach in English, every visitor can hear your words in their own language — live, through their own phone and earbuds. Nothing to install, nothing to hand out: they scan a poster on the wall and tap their language.

The journey of your voice

What Sunday looks like

  1. Before the service — our operator connects the lapel mic and starts the system, silenced. A printed poster with a QR code is on the wall.
  2. People arrive — anyone who wants translation scans the poster with their phone camera, taps their language, and puts in their earbuds. That's all they ever do.
  3. Worship & announcements — the system stays silenced; nothing plays in the earbuds.
  4. You preach — the operator taps one button, and your message flows to every listener in their own language, about 3 seconds behind your voice, with written captions on screen.
  5. After the service — the operator ends the session with one tap.

Where it all lives

Our own server

The system runs on a private server we operate in a professional data center — managed by JC Business Solutions, protected behind encryption and passwords.

AI translation engine

The translating is done automatically by a state-of-the-art AI language model (LLM) that listens to speech and speaks the translation live — no human interpreter involved.

Nobody installs anything

Listeners just open a web page. No app, no account, no personal information collected.

What the church needs to provide

Nothing, really. Each phone uses its own mobile data (about 20 MB per hour — less than a few minutes of video). The church's Wi-Fi is optional — helpful only for visitors who have no data plan.

Usage is measured per minute and per language, and a simple report is available after each service.
